Chrome Extension Policy

Privacy Policy for New Tab Landscapes

Effective Date: September 18, 2026 Last Updated: September 18, 2026

At New Tab Landscapes, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension handles user data, permissions, and third-party services.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

New Tab Landscapes is designed to operate locally on your device with minimal external data requests.

A. Google Tasks Data

If you choose to enable Google Tasks integration:

  • Scope Requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks
  • How We Use It: We access your Google Tasks strictly to display, create, edit, and complete your task items directly within your new tab page.
  • Storage & Processing: All task data is fetched directly from Google’s official APIs to your local browser session. We do not collect, transmit, or store your task content on any external servers owned by us.

B. License & Subscription Data (Gumroad)

If you upgrade to Pro:

  • Information Handled: Your license key is stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local) to maintain your Pro status.
  • Verification: The license key is sent directly to Gumroad's verification API (https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify) to confirm purchase validity.

C. Local Extension Settings

  • Information Handled: Pomodoro timer settings, widget visibility, wallpapers, and feature usage counters are saved exclusively in your browser’s chrome.storage.local.
  • Processing: This data never leaves your device.

2. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

New Tab Landscapes' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • No Server Storage: We do not transfer Google Tasks data to third-party servers.
  • No Human Inspection: We do not allow humans to read your Google Tasks data.
  • No Advertising: Google Tasks data is never used for advertising, marketing, or targeting purposes.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, rent, or monetize your personal data or extension usage data to any third party under any circumstances.

We interact only with the following official third-party APIs as part of core app functionality:

  • Google APIs: Authenticates user sessions and syncs tasks directly between your browser and Google's servers.
  • Gumroad API: Validates license keys directly between your browser and Gumroad.

4. Data Retention and Security

  • All personal data (tasks, settings, timer states) is kept locally on your device or securely within your own Google account.
  • You can remove all local data created by the extension at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing your browser extension storage.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in functionality or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
