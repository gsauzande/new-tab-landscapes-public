# Privacy Policy for New Tab Landscapes

**Effective Date:** September 19, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 19, 2026

At **New Tab Landscapes**, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension handles user data, permissions, and third-party services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

New Tab Landscapes is designed to operate 100% locally on your device with minimal external network requests.

### A. Local Tasks Data
* **Data Handled:** Tasks, to-do items, lists, notes, and due dates created in the extension.
* **Storage & Processing:** All tasks data is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). **We do not collect, transmit, or store your task content on any external servers.**

### B. License & Subscription Data (Gumroad)
If you upgrade to Pro:
* **Information Handled:** Your license key is stored locally in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`) to maintain your Pro status.
* **Verification:** The license key is sent directly to Gumroad's verification API (`https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify`) to confirm purchase validity.

### C. Local Extension Settings
* **Information Handled:** Pomodoro timer settings, widget visibility, wallpapers, and feature usage counters are saved exclusively in your browser’s `chrome.storage.local`.
* **Processing:** This data never leaves your device.

---

## 2. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do **not** sell, trade, rent, or monetize your personal data or extension usage data to any third party under any circumstances.

We interact only with the following external service as part of core app functionality:
1. **Gumroad API:** Validates license keys directly between your browser and Gumroad (`https://api.gumroad.com`).

---

## 3. Data Retention and Security

* All personal data (tasks, settings, timer states) is kept locally on your device.
* You can remove all data created by the extension at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing your browser extension storage.
